## If something is wrong with it

Your Taptile comes with the guarantees in the **Consumer Guarantees Act 1993**
(New Zealand) and, if you're in Australia, the **Australian Consumer Law**. In
plain terms, it has to be of acceptable quality, match its description and do
what we said it does, for a reasonable length of time. Nothing on this page
limits those rights.

If it doesn't:

- **A minor problem** — we'll repair it, replace it or refund you. We'll tell
  you which within [FILL IN: e.g. 5] working days of hearing from you.
- **A serious problem** — for example, it would not have been bought if the
  fault had been known, or it can't be fixed in a reasonable time — you choose:
  a replacement or a full refund.
- **We pay the return shipping** for a faulty unit. We'll send you a prepaid
  label, or refund the postage you paid.

What isn't a fault:

- A keymap or macro you set that stops a key doing what you expect. Resetting
  the board fixes that — see [FILL IN: link to reset instructions].
- Damage from opening the case, re-soldering or physical misuse.
- Normal wear on keycaps and knobs.

## Pre-orders

If you pre-order and we're late:

- We'll email you as soon as we know a date is going to slip, with a new date.
- If the new date doesn't work for you, you can cancel for a **full refund**,
  any time before it ships. No reason needed.
- If we can't ship at all, everyone is refunded in full.

## How to start a return

Email **support@trytaptile.com** with your order number and what's wrong (a
photo or short video helps). We'll reply within [FILL IN: e.g. 2] working days.

## Where to go if we can't agree

You can take a claim to the **Disputes Tribunal** (New Zealand). It's usually
quicker and cheaper than court. Australian customers can contact their state or
territory consumer protection agency.
