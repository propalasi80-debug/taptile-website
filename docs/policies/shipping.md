## Lost or damaged in transit

If it's lost or arrives damaged, email **support@trytaptile.com** with your
order number (and photos if it's damaged). We'll send a replacement or refund
you — you don't have to sort it out with the courier.

## Wrong address

Check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us before it ships
and we'll fix it. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong,
we'll re-send it once you've paid the new postage, or refund you minus the
original postage.
