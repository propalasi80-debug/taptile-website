Privacy.

What we collect, why, where it is kept and how to see or fix it.

The short version. If you join the waitlist we keep your email address. If you order, we keep what we need to send your Dialect and look after its warranty. We run no analytics and no trackers. Some of your information is stored overseas, and we say where. You can ask to see or correct anything we hold by emailing support@trytaptile.com.

Who is responsible

Taptile's privacy officer is [FILL IN: name of the privacy officer. The Privacy Act requires one. It can be the adult on the business]. Contact them at support@trytaptile.com.

No analytics, no tracking

We run no analytics of any kind and no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Nothing about how you browse this site is recorded or sold.

What we collect, and why

If you join the waitlist

Your email address, the page you signed up on and the time. We use it to send you one email when the Dialect goes on sale. You do not have to give it: the site works without joining.

If you order

  • Your name, email address, delivery address and phone number if you give one, so we can send your order and contact you about it.
  • What you bought, when, and what you paid, because New Zealand tax law requires us to keep business records for 7 years.
  • The serial number of the unit we sent you, so we can honour its warranty and contact you if there is ever a safety recall.

You need to give us these details to place an order, because we cannot deliver without them.

Payment details

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us, and we never see or store it.

If you email us

Your message and email address, so we can reply.

Server logs

Our host records IP addresses and basic request details as a normal part of serving pages. We do not use them for anything else.

Browser storage

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. If you join the waitlist, your browser may remember that you did, so the form does not ask again.

Your information leaving New Zealand

Because several of these providers store data overseas, your information is held outside New Zealand. Information privacy principle 12 of the Privacy Act 2020 means we should tell you that plainly, so here it is: waitlist and order records are stored in the United Kingdom, payment records are held by Stripe overseas, and emails pass through Ireland. We keep a copy of our order records in New Zealand as well.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist entries: until you leave the list, ask us to remove you, or we have sent the launch email and closed the list.
  • Order records: 7 years, as tax law requires.
  • Support emails: until the matter is finished and any warranty has ended.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email support@trytaptile.com. We reply within 1 working day and will give you a decision within 20 working days.

If you are not happy with how we handled a request, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Children

We do not check anyone's age when they join the waitlist. If you believe a child has given us their details without a parent or guardian knowing, email us and we will delete them.

If something goes wrong

If we have a privacy breach that is likely to cause someone serious harm, we will tell the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and the people affected, as the Privacy Act requires.

Changes to this policy

We update this page whenever what the site does changes. The current version is always here.
