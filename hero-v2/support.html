Quick start.

Plug it in. It works. Change anything you like after that.

Your Dialect works the moment you plug it in, on Windows, macOS and Linux, with nothing to install. Stuck? Email support@trytaptile.com and we reply within 1 working day.

1. Plug it in

Use the braided USB-C cable from the box and plug straight into your computer. The underglow comes on in white.

2. What the keys and dials do out of the box

123456789
  • Keys: they type 1 to 9 until you give them a job.
  • Left dial: turn for volume, press to mute.
  • Right dial: turn to scroll.
  • Lighting: hold the right dial down, then turn the left dial for brightness or the right dial for colour.

3. Make it yours

Change any key or dial with Vial, a free app, or with vial.rocks in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Your layout is saved on the Dialect itself, so it works the same on any computer you plug it into.

  1. Open vial.rocks in Chrome or Edge, or install Vial from get.vial.today.
  2. Connect and choose Taptile Dialect.
  3. Click a key or dial and pick what it should do.

Vial and vial.rocks are independent open-source projects. Taptile does not run them.

4. Swapping switches

The switches are hot-swap and MX-compatible, so you can pull one out and push another in without soldering. Unplug the Dialect first, check the two pins on the new switch are straight, and press it in gently. Never force a switch.

If the pad stops responding

Unplug it, wait five seconds and plug it back in, directly into the computer rather than through a hub. Try a different USB-C cable, because some cables only charge. If it still does not respond, email us and we will get you going again.
