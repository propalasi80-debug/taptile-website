Returns and warranty.

Seven days to change your mind. Twelve months of warranty. Your consumer rights on top.

The short version. Changed your mind? Tell us within 7 days of it arriving. Something wrong with it? Email us and we fix it, replace it or refund you, and we pay the postage both ways. Email support@trytaptile.com and we reply within 1 working day.

Changed your mind

  • Email us within 7 days of receiving your Dialect.
  • It needs to be unused and in its box with everything that came with it.
  • You pay the return postage. We email you the return address.
  • We refund the price of the Dialect. The original shipping is not refunded.
  • We start your refund within 2 working days of checking the returned unit.

A change-of-mind return is something we offer by choice. It sits on top of your legal rights and does not replace them.

If something is wrong with it

  • Email us and tell us what is happening. A photo or short video helps.
  • We pay the postage both ways.
  • Within 1 working day of inspecting it we tell you whether we will repair it, replace it or refund you.
  • Refunds are started within 2 working days of agreeing one.

12-month warranty

Every Dialect has a 12-month warranty from the day it is delivered. If it develops a fault in normal use during that time, we repair it, replace it or refund you, and we pay the postage.

The warranty does not cover:

  • damage from drops, liquid or taking the board apart beyond swapping switches and keycaps as shown on the support page;
  • custom firmware that drives the underglow harder than the firmware we ship. For example, running every light at full white continuously makes the board hot and is not covered.

These limits apply to this extra warranty only. They do not limit your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act.

Your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act

The Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 guarantees that what you buy is of acceptable quality, matches its description and is fit for its purpose. If it is not, you are entitled to a repair, a replacement or a refund, depending on how serious the problem is. Those rights are yours whatever this page says, they are not limited to 12 months, and we do not contract out of them for anyone.

More about your rights: consumerprotection.govt.nz.

Pre-orders

You can cancel a pre-order for a full refund at any time before it is dispatched, with no conditions.
