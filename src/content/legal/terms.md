This section only applies from the date we start taking orders. It doesn't apply today, because we aren't selling anything yet.

### Your rights under the law come first

Consumer sales in New Zealand are covered by the **Consumer Guarantees Act 1993**, and consumer sales in Australia by the **Australian Consumer Law**. Nothing in these terms limits or excludes those rights, and trying to contract out of them would itself breach the Fair Trading Act 1986. Our warranty is a promise **on top of** those rights, not instead of them. The law can still cover a fault after our 12 months are up, if a Taptile should reasonably have lasted longer.

### What's covered

For **12 months from the day your Taptile is delivered**, if it has a manufacturing fault — a defect in its parts or in how we built it — we'll repair it, replace it, or refund you. For example:

- A key, dial or dial-press that stops registering.
- Underglow LEDs that stop working with the firmware we shipped.
- A USB-C connection that drops out or won't connect.
- A case that cracks or a part that comes loose in normal use.
- The firmware we shipped not doing what we said it does.

If the problem is serious — you wouldn't have bought it had you known, or it can't be fixed in a reasonable time — you choose a replacement or a refund. A repaired or replaced Taptile is covered for whatever is left of the original 12 months.

### Who it covers

Whoever owns the Taptile. If you give or sell yours to someone, the rest of the warranty goes with it. To claim, we'll need the original order number or proof of purchase.

### What you can do without affecting the warranty

Taptile is built to be changed. None of these void anything:

- Remapping keys, building macros, or flashing your own firmware.
- Swapping switches in the hot-swap sockets, or changing keycaps and knobs.

### What isn't covered

- **Physical damage:** drops, liquid, heat, forcing a switch into a socket, opening the case, re-soldering, or other misuse.
- **Custom firmware and the LEDs:** the firmware we ship limits how bright the underglow LEDs can go and how much power they draw. If you change the firmware to push them past that limit and the LEDs or the board burn out, that's damage caused by how the board was used, not a fault in it. Anything that goes wrong for a reason unrelated to the change you made is covered as normal.
- **Settings, not faults:** a keymap or macro that stops a key doing what you expect. Resetting the board fixes it, and we'll help you do that.
- **Normal wear:** shine on keycaps, marks on knobs, and cosmetic scuffs that don't affect how it works.
- **Power from outside the USB standard:** damage caused by a port, hub or charger supplying the wrong voltage.
- **Loss or theft.**

### How to make a claim

1. Email **support@trytaptile.com** with your order number, what's wrong, and a photo or short video if you can.
2. We'll reply within **5 working days**. Often the first step is troubleshooting together — many problems are a setting, and a reset fixes them.
3. If it's a fault, we'll tell you how to send it back, then repair, replace or refund it.

Repairs and replacements may reset the board to the firmware we ship, so keep a copy of your keymap.
