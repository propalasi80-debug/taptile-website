Your Dialect comes with the guarantees in the **Consumer Guarantees Act 1993**. In plain terms, it has to be of acceptable quality, match its description and do what we said it does, for a reasonable length of time. Nothing on this page limits those rights.

What isn't a fault:

- A keymap or macro you set that stops a key doing what you expect. Resetting the board fixes that, and we'll help you do it.

- Normal wear on keycaps and knobs.
